Maxim MAX8690x heart-rate sensor (Samsung input driver)
=======================================================

The MAX86900/86902/86907/86909 family driver of Samsung
(drivers/optics/hrmsensor.c + hrm_max86902.c); the Galaxy Note 8 carries a
MAX86907. It reports as the input device hrm_sensor with Samsung sysfs nodes,
unlike the upstream IIO maxim,max30102 binding. The transplanted parser reads
the stock hrmsensor,* names.

Required properties:
- compatible: "maxim,max86902-hrm"
- reg: I2C address (0x57)
- hrmsensor,hrm_int-gpio: the interrupt line; the driver derives the interrupt
  from it and requests it falling-edge
- vdd-1p8-supply: 1.8 V sensor supply
- led-3p3-supply: 3.3 V LED supply
- i2c-1p8-supply: 1.8 V I2C pull-up supply (kept on once enabled)

Optional properties:
- hrmsensor,vdd_1p8, hrmsensor,led_3p3, hrmsensor,i2c_1p8: the stock rail name
  strings (logged only)
- pinctrl-names: "default", "sleep", "idle"; pinctrl-0..2: the interrupt pin
  states

Example: arch/arm64/boot/dts/exynos/exynos8895-greatlte-l6c.dtsi (hrm@57).
