Samsung GPS standby line (GPS_PWR_EN)
=====================================

The nSTDBY line of the BCM4774 GNSS/sensor-hub combo of the Galaxy Note 8,
exposed as /sys/class/sec/gps/GPS_PWR_EN/value for the Broadcom lhd daemon.

Required properties:
- compatible: "samsung,exynos8895-gps-pwr"
- gpios: the standby line (active high: 1 = GNSS out of standby)

Optional properties:
- pinctrl-names, pinctrl-0: "default" pin state of the line

Example:
	gps-pwr {
		compatible = "samsung,exynos8895-gps-pwr";
		pinctrl-names = "default";
		pinctrl-0 = <&ssp_gps_pwr_en>;
		gpios = <&gpf0 3 GPIO_ACTIVE_HIGH>;
	};
